Title: Principles of Aerodynamics

Aerodynamics is the study of the motion of air, particularly when it interacts with a solid object such as an airplane wing.

Key concepts:
- Lift: The force that directly opposes the weight of an aircraft.
- Drag: The resistance force caused by the shape of the object and air viscosity.
- Thrust: The force that moves the aircraft forward.
- Bernoulli's Principle: Explains how pressure differences create lift.

Applications:
- Aircraft design
- Automotive engineering
- Wind turbine optimization
